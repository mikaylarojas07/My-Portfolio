TriviaTime - Python Quiz Game

For my final project in INFR1101 - Intro to Python, I created an interactive quiz game using Python fundamentals. I implemented features like score tracking, an interactive menu, and multiple categories to choose from.

History of the Smartphone - Paper

The culminating essay for my History of Science and Technology course. In this essay, I traced the origins and evolution of the smartphone, all the way from the Roman Empire and their contributions to computing and mathematics.

Understanding Selfhood Through Literature - Essay

For the final essay in my Reading Our World course, I analyzed how literary works reflect and shape our understanding of selfhood and identity. I supported my arguments using the ideas and frameworks of Max Weber, Georg Simmel, Erving Goffman, and other sociologists.

Leadership Through Fiction - Presentation

This group presentation for my Collaborative Leadership course explores how fictional characters can exemplify different leadership styles and approaches. My group chose to explore how Captain America from the Marvel saga represents different aspects of leadership.
