Overview

For my final project in INFR1101 - Intro to Python, I created an interactive quiz game using Python fundamentals.

Key Aspects

I implemented features like score tracking, an interactive menu, and multiple categories to choose from, demonstrating my understanding of variables, loops, functions, conditional statements, and user input.

History of the Smartphone - Paper

Overview

The culminating essay for my History of Science and Technology course covered the history of the smartphone and the developments that made it possible.

Key Aspects

In this essay, I traced the origins and evolution of the smartphone, all the way from the Roman Empire and their contributions to computing and mathematics. The essay explored major developments in computing, telecommunications, and mobile technology, examining how innovations throughout history contributed to the modern smartphone

Understanding Selfhood Through Literature - Essay

Overview

For the final essay in my Reading Our World course, I analyzed how literary works reflect and shape our understanding of selfhood and identity.

Key Aspects

I explored concepts of identity, social norms, and self-performance using the ideas and frameworks of Max Weber, Georg Simmel, Erving Goffman, and other sociologists. I tied these frameworks into popular literary media works and their representation of selfhood, including The Office, Black Mirror, and Keeping Up With the Kardashians.

Leadership Through Fiction - Presentation

Overview

This group presentation for my Collaborative Leadership course explores how fictional characters can exemplify different leadership styles and approaches.

Key Aspects

My group chose to explore how Captain America from the Marvel saga represents different aspects of leadership. We explored qualities such as teamwork, communication, trust, influence, and leading by example, connecting Captain America's actions to the many leadership styles and approaches.
